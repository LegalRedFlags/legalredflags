# Legal Red Flags

**Spot fraud in legal disputes — before it costs you everything.**

[legalredflag.org](https://legalredflag.org)

A free, non-profit resource built from real experience — not legal theory. After six years, three cities, and five lawyers, we documented every warning sign we missed so you don't have to learn the hard way.

## What's Inside

- **36 Red Flags** — A complete database of attorney warning signs across hiring, retainer fees, representation, and enforcement
- **Real Case Studies** — Anonymized cases covering private account billing, ghosting, lawyer conflicts of interest, and sham bankruptcy
- **Lawyer Red Flag Checker** — An interactive tool to evaluate whether your lawyer's behavior is a warning sign
- **Hiring Checklist** — 23 critical points to verify before signing a retainer
- **Practical Guides** — Word-for-word scripts for refusing personal account payments, demanding refunds, and filing bar complaints
